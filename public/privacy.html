Privacy

Regular QR codes

Your content and icon are processed in memory to make the image, then discarded. Nothing is saved, and there are no accounts.

Editable links

If you turn on "Make it editable", Giraso stores the destination address, when the link was created, and how many times it was scanned each day. We don't store who scanned it, or their IP address, in that record.

You can delete an editable link at any time from its private link.
